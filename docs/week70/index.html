Hawaii Laie Mission — Week 70

Bruce and Sara Conrad, 2026

Sunday (October 18, 2026) morning we enjoyed sleeping in. Next year our ward will meet at 8am, so no more sleeping in Sunday mornings soon! After bathing and dressing we arrived in time for church (at 11:30) and liked both the youth speaker and the high council speaker, who apparently had lived in the ward years before and was emotional about the homecoming aspect of it all. After the second hour, we returned home. Naps ensued, and some conversation with Helaman. We decided to leave for the important meeting in Honolulu tomorrow at 7:45am.

Monday morning, Bruce awoke spontaneously at 5am and went out for an early morning walk around sunrise (6:28am today). Shower and get ready for the trip into Honolulu for an important meeting at 9am. The next meeting had been scheduled for November 25, but today was moved forward to January 25 of next year. Afterwards, we drove to Dr. Lawler's office, where Helaman had an appointment, and we picked up our remaining lab results; Bruce asked her office staff if they could have her finish the forms, but she wants to see us Wednesday morning. At the office we ran into Elder and Sister Hollingsworth and were able to thank him for all of the help he had given Sara with her finance class. We then drove over to Sam's Club and purchased a bunch of food, and drove home. One load of laundry, and naps. Mike texted that he has deposited the October check in Canadian dollars, and that he is going to California for a couple of weeks to visit his daughter. The bank has yet to notify us of the deposit. Watching a movie as the evening wears on after sunset: Enemy at the Gates (2001).

Tuesday morning, Bruce awoke at 5am and went for an early morning walk again; it is very windy. Bruce took a few pictures of some wild chickens that Al fed from chicks; so pets maybe?

We got up and left at 7:25 am, which got us to the mall just a few minutes early for the LTK activity. Then we showered and bathed and dressed for district council meeting. It was fun to meet the six new young missionaries. The Canadian dollar check was deposited, according to the bank, and it is ten percent larger than before, because of the form Bruce submitted the other day, qualifying for a lower tax withholding rate. He paid our tithing for this month.

Wednesday morning, we were both awake at 4:30am and trying to go back to sleep. Didn't work. We got up and went into the ocean for a few minutes, then drove directly to Kaneohe where we showered and dressed and left for the Doctor, arriving fifteen minutes early at 8:15am. Sara got blood drawn and we confirmed that her sugar numbers have improved in the last twelve days. Dr. Lawler filled out our forms. We stopped at Times to purchase a lunch plate of Kalua pork, which was delicious. Home for naps. Bruce drove to the post office and mailed the envelope (at last), filled the gas tank, and stopped at the library to pick up a requested book.

The envelope on its way; hen and chicks outside the post office; art work at the library

We did the weekly puzzles, and Bruce uncharacteristically finished the sudoku first; Sara finished the crossword puzzle first but missed more words than he did. We watched Sabotage (1936).

Thursday morning the alarm woke us at 4am and we got ready to go do early morning seminary security duty. While there, Bruce got to participate in the Hancock cousins' zoom call for a few minutes. At 7am we left and had breakfast and then went to LTK. Bruce called the Utah State Tax Commission to learn that they tried cashing our tax check in April, but it was "reversed" so he called Zions Bank to investigate and they have no record of the check ever being presented. So it's a mystery. Naps, reading, etc. filled the day. In the afternoon we went to Panda Express to join Helaman for a snack. While in the mall we looked for some needed clothing in Ross dress for less. In the evening we attended our shift at the family history center. Watching a movie, Ithaca (2016), which may or may not put us to sleep. It did; don't know how it ends.

Friday morning, Bruce woke up early and called John Balden at 5:23 and talked with him for fifty minutes while he prepared tax forms for us to mail. Sara woke up to text messages at six and texted Bruce, who then came in to finish the conversation (he had been walking to and fro outside to not wake her). Then Sara took a bath while Bruce treated himself to a pizza and papaya breakfast. We got to the mall just a few minutes early for LTK and waited in the car until the mall doors opened. It was great as usual, wearing us out while getting us in better shape. Bruce showered and Sara ate breakfast and then we packed up the car and went shopping at Safeway (Friday is the special sales day and we saved a lot). Bruce was able to print two of the returns but had to run to Longs to buy large envelopes. Two down, two to go. We watched the movie Good Fortune (2025) with Helaman. We cleared off the table so that Sara can begin working on a jigsaw puzzle, and are watching The Love Hypothesis (2026) as the night comes.

Saturday morning, Sara was up working on her puzzle for an hour or so, and Bruce couldn't get back to sleep so he worked on various things: calendaring for the rest of the year, checking bank accounts, etc. Then he crossed the road just before sunrise to see what he could see.

Two views of sunrise from a Swanzy Beach Park bench separated by a couple of minutes

As he was walking he heard a thump and upon his return noted a potted plant on the ground below the balcony above. His assumption was that a cat had knocked it off, but later when talking with Al, he learned that it was probably the chickens. Bruce drove to the chapel to participate in the weekly cleaning that our ward is assigned this month.

Sara fixed a beautiful breakfast with eggs and pork medallions and then we napped. Bruce awoke and prepared to mail a federal tax form (expecting a refund) and a check for state tax, both ready to mail, but just after the post office had closed for the week. We loaded up the car and drove to Kaneohe where we will prepare for and attend the ward Halloween party. Resting. We worked together to build a fruit platter and are relaxing until time to leave for the party.

Cute poem [text below] on tables, just a small laminated card; the parade of costumes

We were asked to help judge the chili contest, and did that before eating, of which we did just a bit too much, but it was all good. Sara engaged a friend with her story of not eating sugar and wheat and her favorite scripture, "Let your hearts rejoice." [2 Ne 9:52] She got the young missionaries involved with him. We slipped out as the festivities continued. Brother States played the role of Lehi at the tree of life at the end of a labyrinth with an iron rod (PVC). and mists of darkness (black plastic sheets and fog machines), etc.

Since this is the time for goblins and bats,

Halloween spirits and ghosts and cats,

Weird happenings and witches brew,

These are the things we wish for you:

May the only ghost that comes to stay,

Be the Holy Ghost to guide your way.

May the only spirits you chance to meet,

Be the spirits of love and friendship sweet.

May the only trick you are asked to do,

Be the trick of helping a friend or two.

These are our Halloween wishes for you,

And may Heavenly Father bless you in all that you do.
