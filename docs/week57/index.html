Hawaii Laie Mission — Week 57

Bruce and Sara Conrad, 2026

Sunday (July 19, 2026) morning came and Bruce went out onto the porch to savor the palm-tree-enhanced atmosphere, not to mention the mountain and clouds.

Sara prepared a thank you card for the Longs, who today gave us avocados from their own tree

The church meeting was great, with a couple of youth speakers talking about their recent FSY experience, the stake young women's president, and concluding remarks from our bishop. Sunday School class was great as always, with Scotty explaining the scattering of Israel. After lunch and a brief nap, we returned for the baptism of Nicole Reyes, by her husband, who will soon be ordained an elder.

Elder Chun, Nicole, Elder Gilmore, and Elder Garner sharing a moment

Monday morning, Bruce was awakened about 2am with some ideas about picos, and worked for a while, with his last post to the issues section at 3:12am. Back to bed and we slept in until about 7am. After breakfast, Bruce drove to the family search room to meet with the Kaonohis and did a dry run of his presentation: "When you have permission from close family, you can do temple work for someone born less than 110 years ago." They liked it. Home for naps and lunch. Helaman drove to a doctor's appointment and was sad when he came home so we had a short talk. In the evening, we tried to join the transfer news but hadn't been invited. We watched the movie Witness Protection (1999).

Tuesday morning, Bruce woke up at 6am to rain. We left at 7:30 and drove to Laie to attend a senior orientation meeting, instead of going to the district council meeting. It was very informative and even inspiring at times. The housing coordinator returned our check. We drove home where Helaman had prepared lunch for us. Sara worked on her class, getting 100% on a quiz. In the evening, Bruce tuned in to a zoom version of the senior devotional and should have awakened Sara who had zonked out.

PCC President Jarod Hester and his wife share a moment during Q&A after presenting

Wednesday morning, Bruce awoke at 5:30 and went outside to catch the sunrise, at 74°.

Photo doesn't do it justice

While Bruce finished up a piece of his life story (February 17, 2014), Sara had telephone conversations with Melanie and our friend, Neva. She and Bruce then prepared breakfast together. Myrna emailed that our cousin John Hancock passed away. He will be missed. Bruce is providing technical support to Sara as she takes the quizzes at the end of chapter five. She got 100%, so we took a break for lunch. Doing laundry today also. While Sara continues to work on assignments, Bruce puts a final flourish into his slide presentation for later tonight and naps. We left for the meeting at 6:30pm and the presentation went really well. After, we were excused and drove to Jack in the Box to order from a coupon we had. The line was very slow, but we got served and home just after dark. Helaman helped us eat the bounty. We are in bed before 9pm.

Thursday morning, we got up and leisurely prepared to drive into Waikiki. We have an appointment with LDS employment specialists at 1pm. We ate breakfast at Pho Station in Kaneohe, and it was very good. Quite the view from the sixth floor balcony.

Wonky collage: a pseudo-panorama from our balcony at Waikiki Grand Hotel

We had parked our car behind the lobby to unload, and then forgot about it. The desk clerk came up to our room to tell us we needed to either move it or pay for a day of parking. So, we left early for our appointment. Because we got there early, we ran into many departing missionaries whom we knew well in Kaneohe over the past year plus. A tender mercy!

Right to left: Sisters Conrad and Anderson; Elders Frogley, Kim, Gilmore, Johns, others

The meeting went well, but we were disappointed Daniel hadn't joined us; it turned out to be a misunderstanding. We drove back, and Bruce dropped Sara off in front of the hotel and went to park on the other side of the zoo. It was a long walk back. We enjoyed a lazy afternoon with some TV and some naps. Towards sunset, we went up to the deck on the top floor and then walked to the beach. Bought a musubi and a bottle of water in an ABC store on the way back. We were so sleepy that Bruce even forgot to do his daily pico tasks. We fell asleep to an episode of Leave it to Beaver, and slept through the night.

Friday morning Bruce awoke at 6:30 and took in the view from the balcony. Happy Pioneer Day! Bruce showered (because part of the deal here is that the hotel is undergoing renovations and the water is shut off every day between 8 and 5) before going into the sea. We didn't go in the morning; we didn't have beach towels in the room. They were just now delivered at 9:30am, as we were eating breakfast. We messaged Helaman about a few things needed for Bruce to join them in a hike to the top of Diamond Head. They arrived at the hotel at 11:38am and picked Bruce up and drove to the state park arriving just before the appointment at noon.

Bruce and Alyssa before hike; Bruce mid-hike (both taken by Helaman); looking back at start

From the top: Waikiki; Helaman, Bruce, and Alyssa, taken by a kind stranger

Afterwards, Bruce and Sara drove to Kapolei (on the other side of the island) to pick up her Jardiance prescription, which was right ready. Bruce went out to fill up the car with gas, and went back in to help Sara finish shopping and get through the checkout. Then we ate and drove home. We went via Kaneohe (which isn't really on the way (but only added 8 minutes to the projected arrival time)) to drop off most of the purchases which Helaman took up to our fridge. On the way, along King Street, we stopped at Jack in the Box to pick up a couple of tacos. Bruce dropped Sara off behind the hotel, and while she took our purchases upstairs, he drove abound the zoo a couple of times and found the very last spot available, as far away as one could possibly be. It was a long walk back to the hotel just after sunset.

The Waikiki Grand Hotel from the top of Diamond Head, and our room from the Honolulu Zoo

Went to sleep at about nine watching the interview of the author of Theo of Golden on Oprah. It took us quite a while to get to sleep actually (the previous sentence was aspirational), as it is quite noisy here outside, including a few fireworks.

Saturday morning, Bruce woke up at 7am. Sara is sleeping still, but has changed into her swimming costume. Received a couple of pictures from yesterday from Alyssa.

On the trail, Bruce was happy to rest for a minute and was holding his hat because of the wind

After breakfast and a phone call from Ammon, we took the beach towels and a boogie board and walked to the ocean, which is really close. We really liked bobbing in the gentle waves of the salt water. Lunch and naps. Phone call from Eli, with much comparison of notes about people he and Sara are related to. Bruce walked to a little place to buy dinner.
