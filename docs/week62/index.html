Hawaii Laie Mission — Week 62

Bruce and Sara Conrad, 2026

Sunday (August 23, 2026) we were happy to be able to attend church again, after a delicious breakfast. Yesterday, we had the good fortune to discover a missing earring on the floor.

The pair: reunited, and it feels so good

After church, we had a wonderful lunch of the soup that Sara fixed yesterday, and rested. Sara had a long phone call with Aunt Janis, and prepared supper. Bruce took a picture.

 

We tried to fall asleep to The Admirable Crichton (1957), but it was engaging enough that we watched it to the end. Watched Rigoletto (1993). Seven Thieves (1960) finally worked.

Monday was a long and lazy day. We are being asked by the mission secretary what we want our departure date to be, since the church has a blackout on travel dates between December 19 through December 26, and our official last day has always been December 24th. We replied asking for an interview with the mission president before we decide on a date. We fell asleep to the movie Splash (1984).

Tuesday morning we ate breakfast, read, played SkipBo, and got ready for district council. While there we were able to get a copy of this picture of the district at the temple sealing outing a couple of weeks ago, on Tuesday, August 11, 2026.

Elders: Walker, Hess, Garner, Guillermo; Sisters: Stacey, Dushku, McCarroll, Tupou, Brenders, Oates

After the meeting, we drove to Safeway to buy the wings that had been rainchecked on Friday, but they still haven't received any chicken; we can try again on Thursday. We then drove to Jack in the Box and ate there. Home for a brief nap, then we needed to leave for the endodontist appointment for Sara in Kailua. While she was being worked on, Bruce went over to the other wing of the building and got his teeth cleaned, and learned that he will need root canals on three teeth. They tried to get hold of Bruce to come and make payment but he was stuck in a chair himself. When he could leave, he returned to sit with Sara while the doctor finished her work. We drove home and Sara put together a nice supper of chicken nuggets, rice with quinoa and lentils, and watermelon. Watching the movie An Office and a Murderer (2012), which is based on a true story. We fell asleep to the movie The Scarecrow of Romney Marsh (1964), also based on a true story, according to Walt Disney's personal introduction at the start of the movie.

Wednesday morning of Helaman's birthday. The inside temperature is 82.6° (whereas last night as we fell asleep it was 85.6° (so the cross-ventilation cooling is working)). We drove to recycle, but were too early, so we went and picked up a prescription for amoxicillin at Target (one dollar).

We made it to the doctor in Waipio a few minutes early. We were standing in the waiting room waiting for a promised cup of water when a woman came in, looked at us quizzically and asked, "Are we standing?" We explained that we had been sitting since Kaneohe, which she said is God's country and Bruce thought she said cross-country, and Sara said he needed a hearing aid. He does but doesn't like it very much. The receptionist came with a couple of small cups of water and we all sat down, and had a nice talk.

The visit was quite brief and we spoke to a PA who told us if the physical therapy place doesn't call us to make an appointment to call them back within the week and they will apply some pressure. We didn't schedule a return appointment because we are now short-timers, counting the very few months until this mission comes to an end. We got gas at Costco and met Derrick (an attendant there) who knows someone who would like to buy the Scion that Helaman has given us the use of, when we return to the mainland. We also shopped after Sara ate a hotdog.

Back home to Kaneohe and unloaded and stashed the groceries. Naps ensued. Helaman returned, sunburned, from a lengthy motorcycle ride. He is preparing for a visit from Alyssa and we are packing for three days in Waikiki at the Ewa Hotel, now a familiar place to us.

Having packed as much as we can, we watched Robin and Marian (1976) to the bitter end and then Sara took a bath to cool down. We started The Scarlet Pimpernel (1982) and fell asleep.

Thursday morning, Bruce woke up at 6am, and let Sara sleep a little. We ate breakfast, finished packing, and drove to the haircut place Sara likes in Hawaii Kai, to try to make an appointment. From there we dropped off some empty cans and bottles ($4) and talked with some hard looking men who had done jail time because of accusations of the baby mamas (as they call them). From there we drove to Waikiki and checked into the Ewa Hotel. They didn't have a parking spot available, so Bruce drove around behind the zoo, parked, and walked back. Naps ensued. Phone calls with Aunt Janis, Eli, and Jessica. We watched All About Eve (1950) to the end, on television (although Sara slept for part of it). Oddly, there were no commercial breaks at all. Nice. Same for The Catered Affair (1956). After that Sara prepared dinner and then we went up to the top floor to watch the sunset and talk. We fell asleep to a sequence of movies. Finally, one started with very loud introductory music and Bruce fumbled for the remote and shut it off.

Friday morning, Bruce awoke spontaneously at 6:25, shaved, and is sitting quietly in the cod chair in the (mostly) dark room waiting for Sara's awakening; she had been awake several times during the night. Our room this time is on the seaward side of the hotel, but on the second floor, so no balcony, and a view only of adjacent parking areas and hotels; the window is smaller and covered with light-resistent curtains. It is also quite a bit quieter than the street side, so we weren't serenaded by garbage trucks. Sara woke up spontaneously just after seven. We had a good time in the water, and visited with a man who was wading with a metal detector. Back in the room, we bathed, then drove to Great Clips in East Honolulu where Sara got her hair trimmed; it looks really nice. Then did a little shopping at Costco and drove back to the hotel.

Back view in mirror on left; which mirror is just visible in the front view on the right

On the way home, Sara ate chicken nuggets from Costco and we looked for the Catholic charity thrift store, but it was closed despite Bruce's phone saying it would be open. At the hotel we watched To Kill a Mockingbird (1962) but both of us dozed off at various times during it. Anyway, it's a classic which we've enjoyed in the past. At 4:30pm we started preparing the salad for tonight; Sara had asked for a bowl from housekeeping and they lent us a couple of Tupperware bins just the right size. When everything was ready, we left and it took over an hour to get to Makakilo for a senior party organized by the Joneses, which was good fun; good food.

Sister Kainoa, Sister Hollingsworth, Elder Nunies, Elder Hollingsworth, Sister and Elder Conrad

It was interesting to us that at our table, two couples were represented of whom one of them was on the mainland; Elder Kainoa is in Saratoga Springs to sign a legal document, and Sister Nunies is in Sacramento visiting a new grandchild. Brother Nunies is missing from the entire group photo because he was the photographer.

Saturday, Bruce watched the funeral service for his cousin, Brent Hancock, at 7am; very touching. Sara prepared a wonderful breakfast. We put on our swimming costumes and walked to the beach and got our fill of floating in the ocean water. Back home for naps and black and white movies and naps. Lunch mid-afternoon was chicken sandwiches. We left at 4:39pm towards Kaneohe and dropped off the contents of the fridge and our dirty clothes and picked up an avocado and broccoli sprouts that Helaman brought downstairs to us. We arrived early to the ward activity. Ate some food, visited with friends, and listened to counsel from visiting Bishop Bruce Vidinhar of the Olomana Ward, about ministering and reactivating. We took a bag of leftover spaghetti and drove to Popeye's for a snack, and were back at the hotel before 10pm.
