Hawaii Laie Mission — Week 63

Bruce and Sara Conrad, 2026

Sunday (August 30, 2026) morning we are preparing to go to Kaneohe to meet with our mission president. It was a very good meeting; he understood what we felt, and we'll work together to figure things out. Sara went home to doctor her headache and Bruce went to church to take the sacrament and to rejoice in the messages given there. Then he asked Destinn to pass around the missionary meals signup sheet and came home to pray with Sara to break our fast.

We returned to the church for choir rehearsal, and feel better about the Hawaiian words that we sing for the third verse and the last two times through the chorus. Home for naps.

The $90 permit that facilitated evening travel Friday and Saturday; Bruce's amalgam fillings

Usually when we stay in Waikiki, we park along the street beyond the zoo, but Bruce wanted to avoid the walk after dark and we had the senior dinner on Friday and an evening ward activity on Saturday, so we splurged for hotel parking for those two nights. It was nice. Sara wants Bruce to have his amalgam fillings replaced, so we took a couple of pictures to count them.

Monday morning Sara woke early, around 4am, and Bruce got a phone call at 5:15am from his cousin John McGilvary, who had heard about the flooding and wanted to know if we were okay; he didn't know about our being away on a mission. Sara had a long call with BYU Idaho and was finally able to get her transcript forwarded to BYU Provo so that the Finance 300 course that she completed at BYU Pathways can be applied to her degree from BYU Continuing Education. So many organizations with BYU in the name, but they are all administered separately, so it can be a challenge to get anything done. Meanwhile, Brce wrote to an official at BYU Hawaii where he would like to teach a course the first semester of 2027, if they want him. We got a phone call from the endodontist who offered Bruce an appointment a week from Thursday at 8am; we accepted. He has two teeth that hurt, upper-left molars.

We drove to Kailua with Helaman, and while he and Sara shopped, Bruce drove on to the library and selected five books. Back at Whole Foods, we all ate pizza and prepared foods, then drove back to the apartment. Laundry, naps, reading, Sara got a call from Eli, and Bruce got text messages from Elizabeth and Kerry Allred. The latter will be visiting Colleen in Cebu, Philippines, and later in the year us again in Oahu. We fell asleep to Pinocchio (TV mini-series 2008); at least, Sara fell asleep before the end of the second (and last) episode. Bruce waited until he had done his 9:10pm work with his picos, reading to pass the time

Tuesday morning, when Bruce woke spontaneously at 6:23, Sara and Helaman were talking in the living room. Bruce emptied the dishwasher and ate some papayas that are nearing end-of-life in the fridge, while Sara took a bath. Getting ready for district council meeting, which was moving. Two of the young missionaries are advancing to a different assignment: Sister Stacey and Sister McCarroll are leaving to become senior companions training new sisters.

Home and resting, reading, doing puzzles, of which Sara finished sudoku first and Bruce did the crossword first. Fell asleep watching Billy Two Hats (1974) with the legendary Gregory Peck.

Wednesday, Sara had a productive physical therapy session at 11am in Kaneohe. After, we put together a nice lunch, then settled in to nap. We received word from Myrna of a visit in Idaho.

Josie and Rachel, visiting Bridget, Elizabeth, and Cody, in Rexburg, Idaho today

Myrna wrote, "Josie and Rachel are at Elizabeth's house tonight. They are on their way to Canada and having a stopover with E." We assume that they are going to visit Josie's brother (thus Rachel's uncle) David and his family near Calgary, Alberta. We fell asleep to Dear Brigitte (1965) with Jimmy Stewart. Didn't work; we watched it to the end, then enjoyed the cool of the evening, and watched Smith! (1969) and then Sara took a bath and Bruce drifted off to sleep.

Thursday morning came and with it worries about Hurricane Lowell which might hit Kauai on Monday (20% chance in the most pessimistic models), and we spent some time watching a YouTube channel showing diagrams from the various predictive models. At 2pm, we drove to a nearby apartment of sisters and filled out the inspection form. Bruce parked incorrectly.

Polite note instead of a parking ticket; phew!

There were naps and reading and laundry. At 5:30pm we drove to the chapel and fulfilled our assignment to host the family search center, where Sara worked on a school paper; no patrons.. Home and in bed by eight.

Friday morning, we drove north to the elders apartment and did the inspection. Bruce scanned and emailed the reports to the mission housing coordinator. Bruce noticed that the machine running his picos had stopped working shortly after 2am Utah time, remotely logged in and restarted it. He read with interest a blog post from Nancy about her daughter Rachel's trip to Alberta. Phone calls from Eli and Tiffany. We tried falling asleep to Renaissance Man (1994) but it was too interesting. Sara did her PT exercises. Trying Patriots Day (2016). We got through that one too, to the bittersweet end, but soon drifted off to sleep.

Saturday is a beautiful sun-filled morning, although dark clouds are low over the mountains. We did a little shopping at Safeway, and then drove to Waipio Costco to get gas and stock up on shelf-ready foods in case the electricity goes out for a long time again. While in Costco, a woman approached us and asked for a blessing for her husband who had an ear infection. We agreed, and after emptying our car at the apartment, we drove to Elder Chun's house and Bruce went with him to Waikiki while Sara drove home. With Elder Chun, we found a parking spot and walked to their rental apartment and gave the blessing. Back home, Bruce and Sara are watching Reacher season 4 episode 1, City of Brotherly Love. Bruce is publishing this post.
