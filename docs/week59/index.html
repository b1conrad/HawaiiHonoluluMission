Hawaii Laie Mission — Week 59

Bruce and Sara Conrad, 2026

Sunday (August 2, 2026) morning, Sara was up first. We had a wonderfully cool night; the new air conditioner works like a charm. After breakfast, we finished our scripture study for the week. Church was good, with moving testimonies. There was a returned missionary, who will speak next week. Porter McCoy told of an early morning motorcycle ride, and returning home to learn that his great-grandfather passed away yesterday at 105. The son of Allan Teruya was visiting from Texas and bore testimony of the church being true everywhere in the world. Noah Fauatea bore testimony of God being in the details of our lives, and the joy of working in the temple (he has been called on a mission to South Africa). Sister then Brother Matavale bore testimony, and the bishopric had him sit on the stand afterwards (rather than returning to his seat (because he has had a stroke and walks with difficulty)) and the last hymn was reduced to a single verse. In Sunday School, we had a new teacher, who taught about Nehemiah being involved in a great work and unable to come down to parley with his enemies. Lunch and brief nap.

We drove up to Laie for the monthly Munch and Mingle potluck; the ukulele group performed.

This faux panorama is missing the group's leader and Sister Cann

We sang God Be with You Till We Meet Again and Aloha Oe to two departing sisters,

here sandwiched between Sister and Elder Sumsion; photographed by Sister Seaman.

Sara drove home in the dark, talking with Melanie, because we met her former Relief Society president, Sister Clark, while walking from the event in Laie. It is so lovely to have a working air conditioner in our bedroom. We had turned it off completely while away, yet it cooled the room down right away when we got home. We are hoping that our electric bill will be lower going forward.

Monday morning, Sara was up first, and opened all the windows and shut off the cooling and drying devices to save power and air out the apartment. It is very pleasant. Then, she went back to sleep. While waiting for her to wake up, Bruce paid our semi-annual car insurance and renewed the annual registration for our motorhome. He took this picture of outside the window.

Roger cleverly painted the hose white against white, making it invisible from a distance

We invited the missionaries in our ward to dinner at Moonga's Asian Bistro. Good times.

Sara, Bruce, Elder Guillermo, Elder Garner, just before Bruce's food arrived

Elder Garner remembered to send us a photograph taken the day of, and just before, the baptism service for Nicole, so this is a brief flashback to week 57.

Elder Garner, Brandon who was about to baptize his wife Nicole, Elder Gilmore

End of flashback. The elders left us a message, based on the experience of Nephi praying on his tower in his garden, as described in Helaman 7:10. They asked us if we have a place to pray where we can feel the spirit. We allowed that we did, but it wasn't visible from the street, and discussed some of these and then it was time to go. We got home in time to watch the next episode of the MAHA movie, about cancer. Long phone call with Eli.

Tuesday morning we got up early and opened the windows for cross ventilation. Bruce read out on the porch and Sara kept busy. We prepared and ate breakfast, then dressed and drove to district council meeting with a box of Books of Mormon. In the middle of the meeting we needed to leave, so gave out some books, and then left at eleven to drive to pick up Denise, getting to her house at 11:30 as promised. We drove her to an appointment at a hospital, the Kaiser Permanente Moanalua medical center. Sara went into her appointment with her at 12:25. The good news is that she doesn't need to have her left little toe amputated.

Having neglected to take a picture with Sara and Denise, we offer the colorful waiting room

We got out of the parking lot without having to pay, because their machines would not read the ticket and so the attendant just let us go. We drove back into Honolulu and ate at the Denny's in Waikiki. Then we dropped Denise off at work and drove home, buying some food at Jack in the Box on the way. Watched another episode of the MAHA movie. Sara finished the sudoku puzzle first, while Bruce was first to finish the crossword puzzle. We are comfortable with just fans to assist in cross ventilation. So nice to have a screened bedroom window that we can open now. We slept through the night with the windows open and no air conditioning.

Wednesday we got up and fixed breakfast and ate while playing SkipBo. We did some scripture study and Bruce cannot talk loud enough to be heard. The study suggested that we read our patriarchal blessings, so we looked for those. Bruce read his out loud. Still looking for Sara's. It was given under her birth name, which she has changed. We read some of her ancestors’ blessings. We made lunch and then after naps watched the next episodes of the MAHA movie. For an hour or so we turned on the A/C in the bedroom, but then turned it off and slept with the window open.

Thursday morning, Bruce awoke about 6am and checked his picos; they had run out of disk space, so he logged in to the server and removed a large log file and restarted the pico engine. We had breakfast and got dressed to go to the zone conference where we inspected a bunch of cars. After that we went into the chapel for a few minutes, missing the picture taking.

Elder and Sister Morris, Sister Martin, Sister and Elder Peterson, Elder Bennett, Elder Jensen

Several of us assisted Elder Jensen, the mission vehicle coordinator inspect a couple dozen mission cars. His grandson, Zander, who is visiting, also helped, and I assume he took this picture of some of those who helped. In the evening, we did our shift at the family history center. Bruce was able to find the signup sheet for August in the clerk's office and enter it into a spreadsheet so that the elders can know where their dinners are coming from. That was a bit of relief, as we had been worried about it since Sunday. Watched Anne of Green Gables. It rained quite heavily just after we got in the house and opened up the windows. Cross ventilation is working really well, and it looks like we'll have another night without needing air conditioning.

Friday morning, Sara had a call (on Facebook Messenger) from Jessica, who is in Europe, having adventures (and working remotely). Bruce wrote and mailed our monthly rent check to the church. We went to inspect the elders apartment and then went shopping at Safeway. Bruce emailed the inspection report to Elder Kaline of the mission office. Note to self: the car needs gas. We drove to Costco in Honolulu and filled the tank. Then we bought a whole bunch of food and drove home. Helaman helped by carrying all of the boxes and individual things up the stairs and into our apartment. We listened to a recording of one of Ron Harper's Christmas programs.

Saturday morning, we woke refreshed by the cross ventilation. Now, after noon with breakfast and lunch behind us, we are enjoying the air conditioning. We watched the penultimate episode of the MAHA movie, about longevity. The background music wasn't quite so bad in this episode compared to some of the previous ones. We had a little discussion about whether or not Ammon lives in Glendale. Bruce decided to present this proof that he lives outside the borders.

The city of Glendale with its oddly broken borders; Ammon's house the grey dot on the right

Now, the day is over, and it's time for Bruce to publish this week's post. Then, Sara has invited him to watch the movie LBJ (2016) with her.
