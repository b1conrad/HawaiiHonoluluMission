Hawaii Laie Mission — Week 73

Bruce and Sara Conrad, 2026

Sunday (November 8, 2026) was a long day, starting with a trip across to Barbara's house where Sara did a little general cleaning and Bruce carted cardboard boxes to the garbage. The dog didn't really cooperate, but we got it done. Then came the general session of our stake conference, which was really good. We didn't arrive in time for a soft chair, but were okay near the front of the folding chairs in the gym. A primary ensemble provided the choir music, and the speakers were all inspirational. At home again, we ate chicken and salad, then napped. The sisters called to see if we could give Jazmin a ride from the mall to the first of two baptisms.

Terrell and E. Petersen; S. Dushku and comp; S. Lahournere and Broderick; Terrell and friends

Terrell is now a member of the YSA branch. He was taught first by Sisters Dushku and Broderick, but baptized by Elder Walker (who—with his new companion, Elder Gibson—recently took over the YSA branch from the sisters).

The second baptism was Anau from our ward, and no general picture was taken, to our knowledge. She was baptized by her husband who was just ordained a priest. Our ward put together a pretty solid meal. A trio of candid photos follows.

Line of members congratulating her; Elder Garner and Elder Guillermo with Anau after eating

Sara had to leave during the first part of the second baptism to give Jazmin a ride and to go pick up prescriptions for Barbara who returned from the hospital around 5pm, but she got back in time for congratulations and a bite of food. At home again and Sara bathed while Bruce put together today's memory-preserving message. We fell asleep to A Knight in Camelot (1998).

Monday morning, Sara went over to get her cell phone that she had left, and neither Barbara nor the dog were awake. Long phone call with Eli dealing with custody issues, then we drove to LTK at the mall. Afterwards, we were anxious for breakfast, which Sara made while Bruce went over to open a medication bottle for Barbara, which required the use of two hands (of which she only has one working at the moment). He had to come back up the stairs to get her house keys because she couldn't open the screen door she had latched (it takes two hands to open, but only one to latch). After breakfast, we dozed off a bit. Bruce had fun reading Phil's latest blog post, Way of the Pico. In the afternoon, Helaman and his friends came by to pick up the motorcycle, and we had a nice long talk with them. After they left, we drove to The Pho Station.

Sara with Bruce who took the selfie; her with pho and him with calamari and avocado shake

He had been helping Easton with a problem from AP Physics 1 over the phone and paused to take the picture. Upon returning home, we were able to back the Scion into our parking spot, since the Indian motorcycle has moved to Kaaawa, thanks to Brent having a license to drive it.

Our apartment in orange; Insets: our mailbox, blue arrow; outgoing, red; parking spot 113

Bruce went out to discard some garbage and took two shots of our apartment from the street, outlining our east windows in orange. Insets show the mailbox cluster, our building 14 address (which is 46-1046), and our parking spot number 113 showing the Scion backed in. Watching a movie, interrupted for a long phone conversation with Eli. The movie is The Yard (2000).

Tuesday morning we were up six-ish. After LTK we ate breakfast and drove to district council.

Sisters Broderick and Lahournere give lesson on Finding; Elders Fonua and Hess demo fishing

The lesson involved the entire zone, so we got to see the young missionaries that we rarely see, and also Elder and Sister Martin. We drove to Elder Chun's house, where he gave us some bananas and celery. Then dropped some books off at the library and Sara selected a couple of new ones to read in the car as we drive. Back home we ate lunch then alternated between cleaning and resting until Helaman brought his friends. We drove them to the airport. On the way home we got steak at a food truck next to Jack in the Box. Nice conversation with the owner. His wife who cooked threw in some garlic shrimp. Very good. When we got home to eat it, Helaman was still there (having gotten a haircut) so we shared with him and he helped carry all our stuff down to the car. Sara and he decided to do some cleaning in his former room. We left following him and parked behind him, unloaded, unpacked, ate, and started watching a movie, The Next Three Days (2010). We abandoned it when Helaman left for his room. The puzzles: Sara finished sudoku; both of us gave up on the crossword puzzle.

Wednesday morning we were both awake before four. We got ready for an over-the-net interview with Bishop Hughes, in support of our missionary application. We had a delightful conversation with our home ward bishop; among other things, he assured us that our home will be taken care of even if Mike needs to move on with his life. Emailed him the message from the dean about the proposed service, and he sent it on to our stake president. We arrived at the temple just after 7:40am and both of us did initiatory work, Sara for her cousin, and Bruce for five temple names. Then we attended an endowment session, Sara again for her cousin, and Bruce for Ulrich Willemann, our (Bruce and his sisters and their first cousins on his dad's side) 4th great-granduncle (a brother of the maternal great-grandfather of Anna Caroline Wintsch (our great-grandmother, who crossed the plains as a young girl, at ten or eleven years of age)).

Several shots of us in front of the temple; middle, Sara holding the card for her cousin's work

Bruce threw a wrench in the works by showing up for initiatory without a reservation, but everyone was most gracious (and the wrench thought is wholly his own; no one said anything), but they were short on workers and in fact three different patrons who were there stepped in as ordinance workers, putting their own work on hold. Time seemed to stand still, with Bruce of course being worried about being late for the 9am session, but he got to the chapel in plenty of time, joining Sara there who had some scripture references on the back of the card and shared them with him. After the session we went to the records desk and got back her card. There we met Kathleen Hallstrom (former temple matron, who knows both our friend Destinn and also Diane (her sister-in-law) who grew up in Taber with us (Bruce and his sisters) in the third ward).

With a new friend, Sister Lei Aken from Makakilo, who took all of these pictures for us

After leaving the parking lot, we went to the distribution center and purchased some children's books on the scriptures for the sisters in our district. We were going to stop at Keneke's for lunch but it was only five minutes more to Kaaawa, so we drove on and had lunch on left-overs at home. Then, finally, well-earned naps. Bruce got another blog post from Phil and spent a little time looking at his work. We finished the crossword puzzle. Movie Hunting Season (2025).

Thursday we woke up with plenty of time to get ready. Bruce walked across the highway to watch the sun rise (which it did behind a dense cloud bank). When he returned, Al was wiping down his car, taking advantage of the soft water of the rain that had fallen. We packed up and got to Kaneohe in time to put food in the fridge on the way to LTK. After breakfast, we alternated between cleaning and resting. In the evening we drove to Jolene's by the Bay to meet with the other senior couples in our outbound areas. Pictures were taken, including this collage of photos that Bruce took from the patio of the restaurant.

The view, from the patio, of the golf course and the bay in the background is quite spectacular

Sara drove to the chapel where we signed in to our weekly shift at the family history center. But then we heard singing in the chapel and it was the choir rehearsal that Bruce had thought would be at seven. So we joined that and then came home. The movie The Maiden Heist (2009) put Sara to sleep, but Bruce watched it to the end and then finished writing this sentence.

Friday and Sara didn't get much sleep during the night, and is not feeling well. Bruce borrowed a plunger from Aaron, and after LTK (which he went to alone (and at which several people missed Sara and charged him to wish her well)), fixed the toilet. Sara had a long phone conversation with her sister, Melanie. We fixed breakfast. There followed bathing and dressing. Photos came.

Sara started shopping at Safeway while Bruce went to the library to return one book and renew another, then went back to Safeway to do the purchasing. We dropped some of the food in the apartment, and some off for Barbara, and returned to Kaaawa. While there, Bruce killed some time getting a tank of gas while Helaman parked his truck in the front spot, so the Scion will be free to leave when we go to church on Sunday. Quiet evening. Watching Righteous Kill (2008). Naps. Three loads of laundry. Naps. The movie A Million Miles Away (2023) was inspiring.

Saturday morning we were awake before six. Sara had slept well, so that's good. Bruce walked over to the sea wall with the intention of watching the sunrise. It was quite rainy and cool; even the chickens had sought shelter. He saw a java sparrow for the first time. Sara fixed a nice ham and cheese omelette for breakfast. We did laundry and some housekeeping, including Bruce hosing off the porch. Lunch was a good soup of chicken and vegetables. Dinner was a cheesy potato casserole. We watched the movie Fatman (2020) on the tv and then a black and white on Sara's laptop to go to sleep. Bruce published this week's post.
