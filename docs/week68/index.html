Hawaii Laie Mission — Week 68

Bruce and Sara Conrad, 2026

Sunday (October 4, 2026) is 22 years from the day we were married: our anniversary!

After carefully listening to the two Sunday sessions of General Conference, we got everything ready for a trip to Kaaawa, where we put the tapioca pudding we made between the sessions into the fridge and organized a few things there and rested until time to leave for the Munch & Mingle senior dinner in Laie. There we met up with Ray Daniel and his wife Gail. Ray and Bruce both worked at BYU in the IT department and were happy to see each other again.

Gail took a couple of shots in a quick hurry, as proof that we were in the same place and time

We left the dinner early because we wanted to get in as much driving as possible before dark; we weren't really successful. Sara drove of course, night falling, and we stopped for a restroom break in Kaaawa. Home uneventfully, in spite of some very bright oncoming headlights. At 9pm there was a zoom meeting about the preparations for the Salt Lake City temple open house. Sadly, Bruce's temporary crown came off late at night.

Monday morning, we got up in time to get to the third and final LTK 101 lesson at the mall. On the way out, Bruce called the dentist and got an appointment because last night the temporary crown fell off; they gave him one at two. We attended the district council meeting, which went well. The stake temple workers were just finishing up a potluck and treated the missionaries to food they had left over. Home to rest briefly. Then off to dentist in Kailua. Shopping at Foodland for sprouts, then dropped Sara off at apartment, exhausted. Bruce drove to library to return books due and select a few more. Sara was sleeping when he got back, so he read a bit and then fell asleep on the porch. Sara warmed up soup for dinner. Then we watched a movie that was quite sad, Savior (1998). Even worse being based on a true story.

Tuesday was a very long day. Up with a 5:30am alarm to get ready and get to where Denise lives in Honolulu and get her to the hospital as early as possible. After dropping her off, we went for breakfast at the Zippy's on King Street near Kalihi, then to Costco to get gas (finding that it is almost the same price as anywhere else). We went to Home Depot to look for a replacement smoke alarm (it has been multiple weeks) and ended up buying some lemongrass plants and a bathmat. Then we got to Costco itself about fifteen minutes before it opened and waited. After shopping, we drove home to Kaneohe and unloaded the car and loaded up the fridge and freezer. Turned around and drove back to the hospital (in Moanalua) and took Denise home via some food for her and Danny at Mac Donalds on the way.

Sara collecting Denise's belongings, and the two embracing in the latter's parking lot

After dropping her off, we stopped at the nearby Safeway to get something to eat, and then drove directly to the endodontist in Kailua, arriving fifteen minutes before Bruce's appointment. Unfortunately, the work was not finished after an hour and a half, and he will need to return next week (or earlier if they get a cancellation). Home and ready for bed and falling asleep.

Wednesday morning we went to LTK and then drove a box of stuff to Kaaawa. While there we went to the beach where we met a retired couple who were watching two grandkids in the water; we talked for a while. Showered and dressed. Bruce peeled and seeded a papaya while Sara put things away. We drove back to Kaneohe for a long nap. We fell asleep to a podcast about the discovery of an old photograph (daguerreotype actually) of Joseph Smith.

Thursday we awoke at about 6am to wind and heavy rain. Bruce reset all the clocks because there had been a brief power outage in the middle of the night. After LTK, we drove to Kaaawa and went into the ocean for a few minutes (walking across grass that had just been mowed (very nice)), then showered and dressed. Bruce set the clocks after the power outage in that place as well. The sisters texted and asked us to come with them on a member visit and we met them in his home at 11:45. It was a fun visit with the bed-ridden musician, who taught us a lot about music and the ukulele; he offered the opening prayer and Sister Oates gave the closing prayer. We had just barely been able to fit that in before a meeting with Craig in Honolulu, but that ended up being cancelled. We had lunch and a brief talk with Helaman, and then napped. Sara is inviting the Relief Society president and her less-active husband to have dinner with us on Saturday, so she wrote out an invitation card; hopefully, we will see her at a meeting in the chapel tonight that happens during our family history shift. If not, we'll have to deliver it to their house. She was there, organizing drawers in the kitchen, and Bruce gave her the invitation. Then we worked on Sara's research paper for her capstone project, with Bruce providing technical support; it is amazing how many little things can cause problems when using a personal computer. Shortly after eight we got home and we are about to sleep. Busy day tomorrow. We fell asleep to David and Goliath (1960).

Friday morning we were both awake very early and especially Sara hadn't slept well. We will drive to LTK at the mall and then from there directly to the lab for blood work for our mission application medical this afternoon. The blood letting was successful and we drove back to Kaneohe avoiding the jammed up freeway on a detour through the university. We had planned to just stay in Honolulu until our 2pm medical appointment but we needed to go home for breakfast and to pick up Helaman for a noon appointment with Craig. After that we stopped at a post office to buy a priority mail envelope and got to our appointment on time.

So many stamps! The postal worker explained that the rates had changed and the available stamps matched earlier rates; $13.90 for delivery in three to five business days. But anyway we mail it ourselves after the doctor gives us the filled out forms and she can’t finish them today because all the results aren’t in yet. Sigh.

Saturday, Bruce got to the chapel shortly before 7:30 am for chapel cleaning. Home to breakfast. We worked hard finishing up Sara's assignments for her Capstone class. About 3:30pm we drove over to Haleiwa Joe's to stand in line.

Sara showing how far back in the line we began; nearly there!

Inside, we were at this large table by ourselves and Sara invited six people to come and join us and we had a delightful conversation with the people from Ohio, including inviting them to come to Salt Lake City next year to tour the temple. They left when the Bennetts arrived.
