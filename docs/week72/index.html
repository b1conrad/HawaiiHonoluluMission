Hawaii Laie Mission — Week 72

Bruce and Sara Conrad, 2026

Sunday (November 1, 2026) at church we had fast and testimony meeting, with some inspirational messages and then at the end a guy who is not a member and it was a little weird and not uplifting. Sara gave a great closing prayer. After classes, we dropped off some things at the apartment and then drove home to eat. Before church, Sara had made a batch of deviled eggs and when the time came we left for the Munch and Mingle event in Laie; good thing we left early because traffic made the trip longer than expected. Good food and company though.

Ukulele group preparing to sing aloha oe to the departing senior missionaries after the meal

Monday morning, Bruce woke up suddenly at exactly 6;30 and walked to the seawall to watch the sunrise, which was veiled from view by the clouds. A brisk wind was invigorating. We drove to LTK, where we met Marynelle Chew, a retired librarian from BYUH who knows Bruce’s sister Myrna. She says in retirement she swims every morning in the university pool and does a lot of temple work. We bathed and returned home and had breakfast, with a little help from Bruce, Sara built a soup which is now simmering on the stove. Laundry, naps, reading.

Tuesday morning, Bruce again woke and walked to the seawall and watched the sun rise behind clouds. We drove to Kaneohe and picked up some drinks from the fridge, and did some clothes shopping at Ross, getting the ten percent discount for older people on Tuesdays. Then we drove straight to district council, which involved food. One set of sisters had a pie that was half baked and opened the oven at the church to see that there are no oven racks. So, we drove to our apartment and brought back an oven rack. Which didn't fit! So, while Sara took a phone call from Jessica, Bruce drove to the Lee's house to borrow one (Sister Lee runs the kitchen and keeps the racks at her home). She asked Bruce to have the young missionaries not eat in the carpeted Relief Society room. When he got back, he put in the rack, told the sisters it was there, and then whispered to Elder Walker (the district leader) about moving into the rooms with tiled floors where we are allowed to eat, and he got that move going. Sadly, Bruce missed a few of the companionship reports with all that. The lesson was given by the elders of our ward.

Collage of candids showing the gathering after the meeting

We returned home and took things upstairs, and Sara bathed while Bruce napped. We did a little bit of work on the kitchen cabinets, then left for dinner with the elders from our ward.

Sara and Bruce opposite Elder Guillermo (back) and Elder Petersen (photographer)

We walked over to another table after the elders left for an appointment and spoke briefly with seven seniors who are leaving for home soon, and had driven down from Laie for dinner together. It being dark, Sara drove home and we went to bed.

Wednesday morning, Bruce watched the sun rise, again behind a bank of clouds. We dressed and packed and drove to LTK. Afterwards we had breakfast, bathed, and did laundry. We visited with Barbara across the way because we are going to watch her dog tomorrow night while she spends the night in hospital after surgery; we have her house keys. Home to naps. Bruce played with Phil's new pico engine version 1.8, and then we made supper and did the dishes. We alternated between two movies, My Mom's New Boyfriend (2008) and The Bounty Hunter (2010) to avoid watching commercials by switching back and forth.

Thursday morning came. Bruce did his usual morning walk, then we packed and drove to LTK. As we were approaching Haiku Point we got a call from Barbara who needed help removing a necklace before surgery and Sara was able to help her with that, on the way to LTK.

The apartment we will soon be leaving, outlined in orange, from two different perspectives

Meanwhile, Bruce carried everything upstairs and got the food into the fridge. After exercising, we returned home for breakfast, we enjoyed a nap, and we bathed and dressed. We spent an hour together organizing the receipts for our 2024 taxes, then went shopping at Safeway and Longs. Resting briefly before going out to host anyone who might come to the family history library for help; no one did. Home after looking in on Barbara's dog, who doesn't seem to have eaten anything. We are now watching the movie A Murder of Crows (1998), which doesn't look likely to put us to sleep; it did not. Trying The Years Between (1946). Sara fell asleep. Bruce was working on his temperature picos and made a mistake. It took him half an hour or so to recover thirty-six lost readings from the logs.

Friday morning, Bruce was awake at 5:30 and couldn't go back to sleep. Sara was up once in the night but is sleeping soundly, which we attribute to broccoli sprouts (which we seem to be out of now). Sara went across the way to check on the dog. After LTK, we drove past Pan's Kitchen but it looked too crowded, so we came home and made breakfast. Then, Bruce showered and we dressed and went shopping in town (that is, in Honolulu) at Down To Earth and Costco. After carrying all the groceries upstairs and putting them away, we rested for a while. Sara bathed, and Bruce worked some more on picos. Helaman is bringing Bruce's suit coat and pants because he is coming here to meet a friend (who took an Uber from the airport). He doesn't want any of the food we bought with him in mind. His friend surprised him by coming to Hawaii unexpectedly, and he drove them up to Kaaawa where they will stay for a few days. We finished The Years Between and started watching Last Chance Harvey (2008). So cute, it didn't put us to sleep. Trying The Guilty (1947) which being black and white just might work. Did.

Saturday morning we both woke up around six and Sara went over to care for the dog, and Bruce helped by walking the garbage therefrom to the Dumpster. Talking over our worries about Helaman. We fixed breakfast, during which we received a photo of the previous district from Sister Tupou, who transferred to another place, but received our request somehow.

Front row: Sisters Tupou, Millward, Broderick, Dushku, Oates, Brenders; back row: Sara, Bruce, Elders Guillermo, Garner, Hess, Walker

We spent an hour cleaning for our eventual departure; Bruce sent the two pictures of the apartment to the next tenants. Sara went and took care of feeding the dog across the way while Bruce walked some garbage to the Dumpster. Long conversation with Barbara on the phone, who will likely be coming home tomorrow, and we are planning to be here for the next few days anyway, so they will have a chance to speak in person next week. Got some stuff in Kaaawa.

Stake conference evening session was inspiring. We had hoped to meet the Longs there, but they came late and left suddenly; maybe tomorrow. We sat behind our friend Noenoelani and next to Elder Chun. On the way into the chapel a man asked Bruce if he was the mission president. We thought about going to the temple every day like we did in Salt Lake City before our first mission, but the hours are not conducive to that project. Watching the movie named Night People (1954). Sara fell asleep, but Bruce stayed up to publish this week.
