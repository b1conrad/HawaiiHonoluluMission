Hawaii Laie Mission — Week 64

Bruce and Sara Conrad, 2026

Sunday (September 6, 2026) morning, Bruce awoke first, to a text message from Elder Chun. Sara slept a little longer, as she had been awake a couple of times during the night. The people we visited yesterday thanked us for our visit and plan on sightseeing today, so he must not be feeling too badly. Church, which felt rushed because of the new meeting schedule. Passed around the signup sheet for missionary meals, but without realizing it had been updated online, so now have a double-booking! We are getting ready for Munch 'n' Mingle in Laie. Didn't make it very far, because the road was closed so we had to turn around, and there wasn't enough time left to get there the other way. We had hoped to get a picture with Elder and Sister Sumsion.

Elder and Sister Sumsion with Elder and Sister Hemsley, all departing September 25th

On Bruce's request, Elder Sumsion sent us pictures of them bedecked in leis as they were wished farewell by the gathered senior couples. Since we weren't able to be with them, we did a bit of shopping at Safeway and spent the evening at home.

Monday morning a bit of sun peeked through from the east, then it became overcast and rainy. We prepared for possible power outages, and waited through the day. We are watching Magic Hour: Lost in the Barrens (TV Movie 1990), which we thought a bit incredulous. As it turned out, the movie Aunt Clara (1954) put us to sleep..The power went off for less than a minute, promptly at 8pm (as Sara had predicted). Sometime during the night, there must have been a burst of wind because our potted mystery plant blew onto its side.

Tuesday morning, we were pleased to attend district council meeting, in which we met the new sisters, Broderick and Millward. We ate crab for lunch: delicious, but hard on the fingers.

Till The Clouds Roll By (1946) was the first movie that we tried this night, but it succeeded not in putting us to sleep. One More Mountain (1994) might do the trick; or not. Did for Bruce, but less so for Sara; nevertheless she did sleep without waking from eleven to five.

Wednesday morning dawned unusually clear and sunny. Rarely do we see no clouds at all.

Keahi a Kahoe in full sun, which is very unusual, with not a single cloud in the sky

A few hours later, it is more normally cloudy. Of interest is that the clouds began small just over the mountain peaks and then expanded from there, rather than blowing in from the ocean. Sara is writing the paper for her last class, and Bruce is checking the mailbox every hour looking for the weekly puzzle; it usually comes on Tuesday, except weeks where Monday is a federal holiday (as is the case this week). The puzzles arrived the last time Bruce checked and Sara finished the sudoku well before Bruce. Still working on the crossword puzzle, but paused so she can enjoy her bowl of stew (Bruce is fasting today (as it is an odd numbered day)). It smells so good! Bruce finished the crossword puzzle first. Watching the convention. It didn't put us to sleep but something less than memorable did in the end.

Thursday, we got up just in time to get to the endodontist by 8am, and Bruce was in the chair getting root canals in tooth 15 taken care of. Then we went to Whole Foods to get sprouts of various kinds and home for lunch. Stopping off at Safeway to return a can of condensed milk that we don't need (the possible disaster having passed us over). We also did some shopping there including cookies that were 75% off. Sara has her next PT appointment at 1pm and we were on the phone with our friend Jacob Siebach to see if we might work out getting together while he is in Laie, but no dice. After our Family Search shift we bought even more of the sale cookies at Safeway and a gallon of milk at Longs. Jacob suggests meeting for lunch tomorrow. We fell asleep to the convention.

Friday morning, Bruce woke up spontaneously at six and read until Sara woke up herself at eight-thirty. She's been sleeping well since discovering broccoli sprouts (who knew?).

Bruce got a call from the folks managing his Canadian retirement funds at 7:30 and made an appointment for the annual review call next Wednesday at the same time (from Lethbridge). Sara was bathing when Bruce realized it was time for us to leave for Laie if we wanted to be at the PCC by eleven, which we did, for the buffet lunch. However, once we got there, we learned that it was only during the summer months and that it was no longer. So we ate at Pounders. We had planned to meet Jacob Siebach there, but his team had brought in lunch to their meeting, so it was just the two of us, with Sara having a Moco Loco, and Bruce the Kailua Pig.

Since we were there, Sara suggested that we drop in on Professor Wolthuis to see what he thought about Bruce teaching for his department early next year, and so we drove over there, and spoke with him. He thinks it is a good idea and referred us to the dean, Aaron Curtis. We ran into Jacob in the hallway and had a nice talk with him until he had to go into his meeting. Since Bruce knew where Aaron's office was (from his last visit many weeks ago), we waited until the last minute to walk over there. Joke on us, because his office had moved to another building! Anyway, he was late coming back from lunch so it wasn't a problem. We had a nice talk with him, and he is excited at the prospect, especially since he found a place where Sara can make a contribution as well, in the math tutoring department. Finally, we dropped in on Elder Seaman, who is responsible for all the senior couples who contribute at BYU Hawaii.

Back home, we rested a bit, and then Jacob stopped at our place on his way to the airport, and Bruce had a nice talk with him. Today is his daughter Katrina's birthday, and he arranged for members in the area she is serving to give her the gifts he had brought for her.

Card, received in the mail, from Florence; wonderful to get a card in the mail—happens rarely

Trying to fall asleep to Mesmerized (1985). Didn't work; some other movie did.

Saturday morning, we both woke up spontaneously at six, and got to work on the day. Sara is making deviled eggs and a potato/cheese casserole for the ward conference breakfast this morning at ten, with Bruce assisting. We got to church on time, and it was a good meal and good talks with various members. Back and Sara arranged with Elder Chun for a stop at Costco on the way to the senior dinner, which we greatly enjoyed. Pictures were taken and will be in the next blog post.
